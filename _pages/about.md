I’m Albert, a postdoctoral researcher at the Universitat Oberta de Catalunya (UOC), where I work  in the [Research Group in Cognition and Language (GRECIL)](https://grecil.uoc.edu) on how multimodal cues shape communication in neurodevelopmental conditions.

I study language development, pragmatics, and multimodal communication, specifically how spoken prosody and co-speech gestures interact to convey speaker intent. For my PhD at UOC, I used behavioral and eye-tracking paradigms to investigate how children with Developmental Language Disorder (DLD) integrate prosody and gestures to resolve phrasal ambiguities and infer non-literal meanings in real time. Currently, my research focuses on how autistic children process multimodal pragmatic cues and how parents adapt their speech and gestures when interacting with them.

During my thesis, I also spent time as a visiting researcher at the Center for Advanced Research in Education (CIAE) at the Universidad de Chile and at Utrecht University. Earlier in my training in Psychology and Primatology, I conducted research on great ape behavior and personality at the Max Planck Institute for Evolutionary Anthropology in Leipzig.

Alongside my research, I teach courses on language acquisition and research methods at UOC and Universitat de Girona. You can find my full CV [here](/files/cv.pdf), and feel free to reach out via email if you’d like to chat about language, gestures, or any topic!
